# Privacy Policy for Photo Frame

Last updated: February 25, 2026

## Data Collection

Photo Frame does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## Photo Access

- The app only accesses photos you explicitly select through the system photo picker
- Selected photos are stored locally on your device within the app
- Photos are shared between the main app and widget through iOS App Groups (local device storage only)
- No photos are uploaded to any server or transmitted over the internet

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device:
- Selected photos are saved in the app's local storage
- Pro upgrade status is stored in UserDefaults (local only)
- No cloud storage or external servers are used

## Third Parties

Photo Frame does not:
- Use analytics services
- Include advertising SDKs
- Share data with third parties
- Use tracking technologies

## In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store:
- We do not have access to your payment information
- Apple handles all payment processing
- Purchase verification is done through Apple's secure servers
